# Code of Conduct

This is a learning space. Everyone here is either a beginner or was one not long ago.

**We expect everyone to:**
- Be kind and patient — explain, don't mock
- Give feedback on the code, not the person
- Respect other people's work and folders
- Ask questions freely and welcome questions from others

**Not acceptable:** insults, harassment, discriminatory language, or spam.

Anyone who breaks these rules may have their comments removed or be blocked from the repository.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer at **codewithaddo@seaddoitech.com**.
